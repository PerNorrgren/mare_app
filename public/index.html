The Book Companion

The story comes alive while you read

You read the book aloud, from paper or your e-reader. Your child follows along on a tablet, and the Whispering Woods come to life:

  • ✨ Magical pictures for every chapter, full of glowing secrets to find: tap them to hear the church bell, the ticking clock, the puppy, or Mare herself.
  • 💬 Talk to Mare about the picture: ask her how she felt, why she couldn't sleep, what helped her. She only knows the story up to your chapter, so there are no spoilers.
  • 📖 You're the guide: choose the chapter on your phone and the pictures on the tablet follow by themselves.
  • 🌿 A small calming practice for every chapter, the same ones Mare uses in the story, to try together in a minute or two.
  • 💌 Write to Mare, and she writes back.
