Words waiting to be read

Nothing a child writes appears in the forest until you approve it here. The app pre-reads each word: "check" means look closely (personal details, unkind words, or a child who might be upset). You can fix a typo before approving.

Loading…

Mare's monthly questions

Two kinds, both on the Club Mare page. Whisper Word: children plant one word, the words grow in the forest, and at the end of the month you choose the Whisper Word. Whisper Question: an imaginative question children answer in a sentence; approved answers appear under it. One of each can be open at a time — close the old one when you open the next.

Riddles from the Whispering Forest

The monthly book treasure hunt. Clues and the secret code are checked on the server, so answers and the promo code can't be found in the page. Put [PROMOCODE] in the reward text where the code should appear — and make sure that code exists as an offer in Sales & Marketing. Open one riddle at a time.
